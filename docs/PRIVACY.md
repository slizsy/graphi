# Privacy

Short version: Graphi doesn't collect anything.

- There are no accounts, servers, analytics or ads. Graphi never sends data anywhere.
- The texts you save, your settings, and your session reports stay in your browser's extension storage on your own computer. Uninstalling Graphi deletes them.
- Graphi only runs on a page when you start it there, by clicking its icon or using one of its shortcuts. It doesn't read pages in the background.
- In private or incognito windows it saves no reports, statistics or page addresses unless you turn that on in settings.
- The optional "all sites" permission is only used to reach editors inside embedded frames, and only when you start typing.

Questions? Open an issue on the GitHub repository.
